# Foodcube

*"It's edible. Probably."*

Adds the legendary **Foodcube** from *Kenshi* to **Vintage Story**.

## Crafting

* 1× Dough
* 8× Vegetables (Times 2 for Fennels)

Bake it into a dense, nutritious brick of agricultural efficiency.

## Why?

Because sometimes you don't want fancy meals.

Sometimes you want to stare into the wilderness while chewing on a compressed cube of vegetables and questionable life choices.

## Features

* Foodcube
* That's it.
* It's a cube.
* Of food.
